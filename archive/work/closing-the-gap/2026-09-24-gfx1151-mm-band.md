## 2. The fix — widen the dense arm to RDNA3_5

`0028`'s dense gate is `GGML_CUDA_CC_IS_RDNA4(cc)` in `ggml_cuda_mul_mat` and the pair-fusion
stand-down.  Widening it to `(RDNA4 || RDNA3_5)` (experiment branch, A/B via the existing
`GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_MMVQ_DENSE_BAND` kill-switch), qwen4exp S_TG:

| config | B8 | B9 | B10 | B11 | B12 |
|--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| dense off, MoE off | 95.5 | 89.4 | 96.0 | 96.7 | 102.1 |
| dense off, MoE on | 95.5 | 85.9 | 91.5 | 90.6 | 93.7 |
| **dense on, MoE off** | 95.2 | **98.0** | **104.9** | **104.1** | **109.4** |
| dense on, MoE on | 95.8 | 93.5 | 98.4 | 97.1 | 100.0 |

The dense widening removes the dip and gives **+9.6 % B9, +9.3 % B10, +7.7 % B11, +7.1 % B12**, B≤8
untouched.  Final default (dense on, MoE arch-gated off), two rounds:

| B | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 |
|--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|

| delta | −1.0 % | +0.9 % | +0.2 % | **+10.0 %** | **+9.9 %** | **+7.6 %** | **+8.7 %** |

## 3. The MoE arm — a loss on gfx1151, across routed types (§4.5)

The routed band→16 (`MMVQ_MOE_MAX_BATCH_SIZE`, `mmvq_mmid_max_batch_band` floor) **fires** on gfx1151
and replaces the gfx1151-tuned routed-compact MMQ (`mmq_rdna3_5_id_get_J`) with the
one-warp-per-token `mul_mat_vec_q_moe` at B=9..16.  Per-type A/B (dense band on in both arms; MoE on
vs MoE off; two rounds; S_TG delta at B=9..12):

| routed type | B9 | B10 | B11 | B12 |
|--|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 35B-A3B UD-Q3_K_M | +3.5 % | +1.3 % | +0.2 % | −0.9 % |
| 35B-A3B Q4_K_M | −2.2 % | −2.9 % | −3.9 % | −4.3 % |
| 35B-A3B Q4_1 | −2.2 % | −2.7 % | −3.5 % | −4.4 % |
| 35B-A3B UD-Q5_K_M | −3.9 % | −5.0 % | −6.3 % | −7.1 % |
| 35B-A3B True-Q3_K_M | −1.2 % | −2.4 % | −4.7 % | −5.6 % |
| qwen4exp IQ4_XS | +0.2 % | −4.7 % | −5.8 % | −7.9 % |
| qwen4exp IQ4_NL | −3.5 % | −5.6 % | −6.7 % | −8.0 % |

Nearly every routed type regresses; only the UD-Q3_K_M model is neutral/positive.  A per-type cap
would therefore have to exclude almost everything, so this is a **per-arch** decision, not a per-type
table: **floor the RDNA3_5 band at `MMVQ_MAX_BATCH_SIZE` (the pre-extension 8-wide band) and leave
RDNA4 at `MMVQ_MOE_MAX_BATCH_SIZE`.**  `GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_MMVQ_MOE_BAND` is a no-op on RDNA3_5 from
`0031` on.

`0031` also splits the device `get_mmvq_mmid_max_batch_for_device()` `RDNA3_5` branch from `RDNA3`
for consistency (that helper is currently unused).

## 5. Verdict

* **Dense odd-row band: widen to RDNA3_5** — clear win (+7..10 % at B≥9), no W≤8 change; 27B dense
  and 35B-A3B controls unaffected.
* **Routed MoE band: keep the 8-wide band on RDNA3_5** — the extension is a loss for almost every
  routed type; gfx1151's tuned routed MMQ already wins.
* **RDNA3_0 (gfx1100) is still open** — evaluate the dense widening and the band floor separately on
  `fingon` per [`gfx1100-closing.md`](gfx1100-closing.md); `0031` deliberately does not touch it.
* **RDNA4 is unchanged** — `0028` stays as measured on gfx1201.
