# A scheduling puzzle

Read the whole problem before you start. Work through it step by step, and state your reasoning
explicitly at every stage. Only give the final timetable at the very end.

Four engineers, Ada, Bruno, Chen and Dara, must each spend one full day with a single shared test rig
during a five-day week (Monday to Friday). Exactly one engineer uses the rig per day, and one day of the
week is left unused. The following constraints all hold:

1. Ada must go before Chen, but not on Monday.
2. Bruno must go on the day immediately after Dara.
3. Chen cannot go on Friday.
4. The rig must be left idle on the day immediately before Ada goes, so that the calibration drifts
   back to nominal.
5. Dara must go after at least one engineer, and before at least one other engineer.
6. Bruno and Chen must not be adjacent.
7. The idle day must not be immediately adjacent to the weekend at either end, meaning it is neither
   Monday nor Friday.

Work out every assignment of engineers to days that satisfies all seven constraints. For each valid
assignment, say which day is idle. Then answer these follow-up questions:

- How many distinct valid assignments are there?
- Which engineer or engineers, if any, is/are on the same day in every valid assignment?
- If constraint 7 is removed, how many additional assignments become valid, and what changes?

Finally, suppose the rig is upgraded so that two engineers can use it on the same day provided they
work in different halves of the day, and exactly one of the five days still has no users at all. Re-solve
the problem from scratch under that change, list the valid schedules, and explain where the previous
symmetries break.
