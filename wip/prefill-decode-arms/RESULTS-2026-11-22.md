# 2026-11-22 — pass 2: the §3.4 "residual" is TWO effects with one hash, and the censuses that prove it

**Verdict.** The residual of `RESULTS-2026-11-21.md` §3.4 item 3 (R1 warm reads `b79e73e3fe9f` against the
reference's `d5083bd41b4c`) is **not** the recurrent tail and **not** the take: it is **two independent effects
that happen to land on the same text**.

1. **At full residency it is the hipBLASLt solution path.** With `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0` the two-arm run is
   **bit-identical to the single-context reference in every sampled row of every rep** (480/480 logits rows,
   `max|Δ| = 0.0`), warm reps included. Without the pin, R1's divergence is already present in **R1's prefill last
   row** (`rep 1, req 1, step 0`) with `max|Δ| = 2.39` and `mean|Δ| = 0.35` — an O(1) difference, not rounding —
   and it appears only from the first rep *after ctx B has decoded once*, then stays fixed (reps 1 = 2 = 3).
   Pass 1's full-residency oracle block did not carry the pin (`RESULTS-2026-11-21.md` "Reproduce"); r310 already
   requires it on a text arm, so this is an omission that manufactured a "residual".
2. **On the host tier it survives both pins** and is a **residency-class** effect
   (`GREEDY-PURITY.md` §89): the two runs' own censuses differ (`h=0.8161` vs `0.8172`). So pass 1's §3.3
   "refutation of the host tier" is itself refuted — what full residency reproduced was effect (1), not this.

**And the two-cursor finding of §3.4 is confirmed, and is sharper than recorded**: with the cells' allocation
state reset at the rep boundary the arms' KV layout is **identical to the single-context reference** (0 of 243
census lines differ, on both shapes, in every rep), while **without** the reset **192 of 243** lines differ and a
warm rep places a sequence's decode token in a cell *below* its own prompt (pos 512 in cell **0** while the prompt
occupies 512–1023) — a scatter, not a corruption, and it moves the numerics.

All arms: 1 × R9700 (gfx1201), ROCm 10.1.0-gfx120X, **sequential**, `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`.  Two shapes:
**full residency** (`-ngl 99 -c 8192 -b 2048 -ub 512`) and the **product shape** (arena 5000,
`-ncmoe 40 -lme auto -c 16384 -b 2048 -ub 512`), scenario `512:192,2048:48`, `ARMS_WAIT_ALL=1` (no overlap),
`ARMS_RESET_CACHE=1` where stated.  Raw logs + `compare.py`: [`runs/diag-cells-2611/`](runs/diag-cells-2611/).

## 1. The instrument (what this pass added)

Three diagnostics, all of which cost nothing when off, and all of which a later arm or the server cut can reuse:

| where | knob | what it prints |
|---|---|---|
| `llama-kv-cache.cpp` (`find_slot`) | **`LLAMA_KV_DEBUG_CELLS=1`** | one line per ubatch/sequence: `seq, strm, n_tok, head_in` (the cursor the scan started from), `used`, `size`, the sequence's `pos` range and the cells as **runs** (`runs=0-511`) |
| `llama-memory-recurrent.cpp` (`find_slot`, `seq_state_take`) | **`LLAMA_RS_DEBUG_CELLS=1`** | `head, n, min, max, rs_z, used, size` and per cell `idx:pos/src/src0/tail/seq ids`, plus every sequence's sentinel `tail/pos`; the `took` variant prints the destination rows right after a handover |
| `arms/two_arms.cpp` | **`ARMS_LOGITS_DIR=<dir>`** | every sampled row as raw `f32` (`<mode>-r<rep>-q<req>-s<step>.f32`) + `manifest.txt` with the row's sha256, argmax and top-5 |

`LLAMA_KV_CACHE_DEBUG=3` already existed and prints the whole cell map, which is 64+ lines per ubatch at `-c
8192` and cannot be diffed; these are one line each.  The `.f32` bodies are **not** committed (1.8 GiB); the
manifests are, and `runs/diag-cells-2611/compare.py` re-runs both comparisons from the logs.  WIP branch commit
`c49584c60` (5th commit of `wip/prefill-overlap`).

**The 2 × 2 that answers the question** (full residency, 2 reps each; reference `batch-pair` =
`b0281be7c5a6` / `d5083bd41b4c`, stable in every rep):

| arm | `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0` | `ARMS_RESET_CACHE=1` | R0 (512 tok / 192 gen) | R1 (2048 tok / 48 gen) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `batch-pair` (one context, 2 slots) | — | — | `b0281be7c5a6` ×2 | `d5083bd41b4c` ×2 |
| `arms` | no | **yes** | `b0281be7c5a6` ×4 | rep 0 match; reps 1,2,3 **`b79e73e3fe9f`** |
| `arms` | **yes** | **yes** | `b0281be7c5a6` ×2 | **match ×2** (bit-identical rows) |
| `arms` | **yes** | no | rep 0 match, rep 1 **`e05c6e16a136`** | rep 1 **`abe253ea957a`** |
| `arms` | no | no | rep 0 match, rep 1 **`e05c6e16a136`** | rep 1 **`abe253ea957a`** |

Read the two axes: the **pin** owns `b79e73e3fe9f` (row 2 → row 3); the **allocator state** owns
`e05c6e16a136`/`abe253ea957a` (row 3 → row 4), and rows 4/5 are numerically identical to pass 1's no-reset table.
The two effects are **orthogonal** and were previously superimposed.

## 2. Effect 1 — full residency: the hipBLASLt path, already a project rule

Logits, `batch-pair` vs `arms` (full residency, reset, no pin), 480 rows per arm (`1 + 191` for R0, `1 + 47` for
R1; step 0 is the **prompt's last row**, i.e. prefill logits):

| scope | rows | differ | first | what it means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rep 0, both requests | 240 | 0 | — | the cold rep is bit-identical, as pass 1 said |
| rep 1, R0 | 192 | **0** | — | R0 warm is bit-identical too: the *take* and *decode on ctx B* are not the carrier |
| rep 1, R1 | 48 | **48** | `req 1, step 0` | the divergence is in **R1's prefill**, before any decode step of that rep |

Numbers on that first row: `max|Δ| = 2.385`, `mean|Δ| = 0.354`, `argmax 266 = 266`, KLD `2.2e-05`; step 1
KLD `1.4e-03`; step 47 `argmax 408 → 159034`, KLD `23.3` (where the greedy text finally flips).  With
`ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0` the same comparison reads **0 of 480 rows differ, `max|Δ| = 0.0`** on the very row that
differed (`rep1 req1 step0`, `np.array_equal` true).

So: R1's *own* warm prefill on ctx A gives a different answer once ctx B has decoded once.  Nothing about the
handover (`seq_state_take`), the recurrent tail, or the cursors is involved — those rows are the prefill's own.
The trigger is the second context's first decode, the effect is stable afterwards (reps 1–3 identical), and it
disappears with the hipBLASLt path disabled.

**New data point for the §44 class**: the hipBLASLt choice can change **mid-process**, not only per start — so
"the same process" is not a purity guarantee for a text arm that adds a second context/stream.  `r310`'s pin is
mandatory on every text arm, full residency included (pass 1's oracle block omitted it).

## 3. Effect 2 — the host tier: a residency class, not a layout

Product shape, **both pins on** (`MOE_EXPERT_HEATMAP=load`, `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0`), `ARMS_B_BATCH=4`,
`ARMS_RESET_CACHE=1`, 4 reps:

| arm | rep 0 | rep 1 | rep 2 | rep 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `batch-pair` R0 / R1 | match / match | match / match | match / match | match / match |
| `arms` R0 / R1 | match / match | match / **`b79e73e3fe9f`** | match / **`b79e73e3fe9f`** | match / **`b79e73e3fe9f`** |

Stable, not a warm-up transient: the arms settle into a *different steady state*, and the run's own residency
census says why (same session, same config):

| census | `arms` | `batch-pair` |
|---|---|---|
| arena residency | `h=0.8161` (738846/905392 reaches) | `h=0.8172` (739891/905392) |
| fills / evictions | 35014 / 26854 | 34984 / 26824 |
| takeover / `get_ok` | 91704 / **99224** | 91704 / **1424** |
| heat coverage | 73.69 % of the routed mass | 74.53 % |

That is the `GREEDY-PURITY.md` §89 class name for name: residency selects the MoE kernel set and the sets are not
bit-identical.  Two contexts warm the 5 GiB arena differently from one, so the *two-arm* run sits in a different
kettle than the reference — which makes a cross-configuration greedy hash an invalid oracle **on the tier**, for
this reason (not for the cursor reason pass 1 named).

## 4. The two-cursor finding (§3.4) is confirmed — and it is a scatter

KV cell census, `[TAG_KV_CELLS]`, aligned per ubatch:

| comparison | lines | differ |
|---|---|---|
| `arms` (reset) rep 0 vs rep 1, full residency | 243 | **0** |
| `arms` (reset) rep 0 vs rep 1, host tier | 243 | **0** |
| `arms` (reset, rep 1) vs `batch-pair` (rep 1), full residency | 243 | **0** |
| `arms` (reset, rep 1) vs `batch-pair` (rep 1), host tier | 243 | **0** |
| `arms` (**no** reset) rep 0 vs rep 1 | 243 | **192** |

Without a shared allocation state the warm rep does this (R0, the 512-token request, first decode step):

```
rep 0:  seq=0 strm=0 n_tok=512 head_in=0   used=0   runs=0-511      # prompt
        seq=0 strm=0 n_tok=1   head_in=512 used=512 runs=512        # decode token, right after it
rep 1:  seq=0 strm=0 n_tok=512 head_in=512 used=0   runs=512-1023   # prompt moved up (ctx A's own cursor)
        seq=0 strm=0 n_tok=1   head_in=0   used=512 runs=0          # ctx B's cursor is 0 -> the cell at the ARRAY TOP
```

Cell 0 is **empty** there (freed when rep 0's request completed), so the scan takes it legally — but the sequence's
cells are now non-contiguous, its `n_kv` grows to cover both ends, and the tile-FA split (index-dependent,
`scripts/gate-spec-purity.sh`) lands elsewhere.  That is the mechanism §3.4 named, measured directly for the first
time, and it is exactly what one allocator for the pair (T0) removes: with the cursors in one state the layout is
byte-identical to the single-context reference on **both** shapes.

Not a carrier, now excluded directly: the **recurrent** cell index.  `[TAG_RS_CELLS]` shows the arms place R1 in
recurrent cell **0** (`used=1`) where the single context places it in cell **1** (`used=2`), and step 0 still reads
identically (`rs_z` in steady state is `-1`; every taken row has `src = src0 = its own index`; the graph zeroes
`rs_z`'s row before the gather).

## 5. What this changes

* **T3's oracle (the maintainer's question 2) is now half-answered by measurement**: at **full residency /
  no-host-tier**, with `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0`, the strong oracle — a cross-configuration greedy hash, `arms` vs
  `batch-pair`, 2 slots, an MoE model, warm reps — is **available today** (480/480 rows bit-identical), with
  `ARMS_RESET_CACHE` standing in for the allocator T0 will build.  So: build T0, then re-run this exact 2 × 2 and
  drop the harness control.
* **On a partial host-expert arena the hash stays invalid** (`b79e73e3fe9f`, stable, with every pin): the verdict
  there is `scripts/gate-prefill-logits.sh` (KLD) plus text compared **within one residency class**.
* **`ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0` goes into every text arm, full residency included** — and the campaign's reproduce
  block is corrected (below).  A second context can move the library's choice mid-process.
* **T0's justification is unchanged in kind and stronger in degree**: §3.1's double-allocation window stays a
  hazard by construction, and the scatter above is its visible form (the decode arm's cursor does not know what the
  prefill arm took, so a warm run reuses the low cells while the prompt sits above them).
* Nothing was applied to `patches/`, no block was created or amended, no tag was cut; `~/llama.cpp` carries the
  censuses on `wip/prefill-overlap` (5 commits, clean).

## Caveats

* The logits comparison is 2 reps × 2 arms per shape; the 4-rep runs (stability of `b79e73e3fe9f`) carry text
  hashes only, and the host-tier 4-rep runs carry no cell census (the censuses are from the 2-rep pairs).
* One process per arm, arms sequential — never in parallel (`HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`).
* The hipBLASLt mechanism is **not** identified (workspace/stream/heuristic re-pick): what is measured is that the
  flip needs the second context's first decode, is stable after it, and vanishes with the path disabled.
* `ARMS_RESET_CACHE` wipes *both* contexts' cell metadata and cursors at the rep boundary; it is a harness control,
  not the feature.  T0's publish/reserve is the product form.
* Full residency here means `-ngl 99` with all experts in VRAM (zero gathers); the host-tier arms are the product
  shape (arena 5000, `-ncmoe 40 -lme auto`), whose cold rep costs ~4.1 s per 512-token prompt (fill-dominated, as
  ever — rep 0 is never quoted as a rate).

cd ~/llama-cpp-rdna-boosts/wip/prefill-decode-arms/arms && ./build-two-arms.sh

M=/llm/models/Qwen3.6/35B-A3B/Q4_K_M/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q4_K_M.gguf
