## Interpretation

- **Prefill**: the sparse-attention crossover sits between ~16K and ~32K context on gfx1201
  (dense ahead ≤16K, sparse +14.5% at 32K, +48% at 64K).  QSA default-ON is correct — a
  QSA_OFF default would crater prefill at 32K+ contexts.  The depth-0 QSA_OFF "win" earlier
  in the session was a short-context artifact.
- **Decode**: sparse decode loses with depth (dense +20% at 32K, +29% at 64K, flat at d0).
  The per-token indexer store/scoring + sparse-FA decode path does not beat dense attention
  on gfx1201 the way the gfx1151 campaign's QSA decode tuning made it win there — the
  RDNA4 sparse decode kernels need their own tuning (indexer store/scoring kernels +
  sparse-FA decode launch geometry / selection width per arch).
