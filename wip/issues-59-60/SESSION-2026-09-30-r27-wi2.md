## 2. The #60 fix

The reporter's data on the r21 head: default fails at `-p 196608` while `LLAMA_QSA_SCORE_WMMA=1`
(forced fused) passes at 883.6 t/s, and `LLAMA_QSA_SCORE_WMMA=0` (forced chain) also fails.
`180000` passes, `196608` is the marginal fail.  Their geometry gives
`score_bytes = n_kv * 1024`, so the old 128 MiB auto switch was at `n_kv = 131072`.

**Why the old threshold failed.**  Below its own 128 MiB chunked branch the unfused chain runs the
unchunked `mul_mat` and a separate `relu`, so its reserve peaked at twice the score size just under
the cutoff, and the chunked chain above it still reserves more than the fused op.  The default's
reserve was therefore the chain peak, and the 196K prefill crossed the reporter's headroom.

**Changes:**

* Auto's fused switch is now `score_bytes > LLAMA_QSA_SCORE_WMMA_MB` MiB, default **64**.  On their
  `r=4` geometry that is `n_kv` about 64K.  It keeps the chain at their d30K and d64K measurement
  points (d64K is 62.5 MiB) and switches to the fused op before the chain's reserve peak grows into
  the 196K ceiling.  `LLAMA_QSA_SCORE_WMMA_MB=0` means always fused; a positive value tunes the
  crossover without a rebuild.
* The unfused chain's `mul_mat+relu` (unchunked) and chunked `sc=relu(sc)` now use
  `ggml_relu_inplace`, so the chain never holds the score and its relu at once.  This is
  bit-identical and removes the chain's 2x-score reserve peak; it is the "shrink it" half of the fix
  and helps the forced-chain path too.

Full diff versus r27: the 8 files of the original fix plus the two `ggml_relu_inplace` lines and the
threshold/knob in `qwen4exp.cpp`.
