The coalesced arm is stable (292-321).  The per-expert arm is **bimodal (280 vs 81)**, and the mechanism is the
drive: a 0.65 MiB O_DIRECT read is ~0.65 ms warm but ~10 ms on the first touch after idle, so a path that issues

a single SPAN=0 arm; quote the pair.

**Finding 1 — the demote costs ~53 % of the first-pass prefill on the field shape, and buys nothing for decode.**
SINK=0: 1762.8 / 1865.3 / 1883.9 (mean **1 837**), decode 75.8-77.9.  SINK=1: 855.5 / 818.8 / 928.4 (mean
**867**), decode 76.1-76.2.  The census names the mechanism: the pool's **"neither tier"** volume — the prefill's
per-ubatch re-read of the experts no tier holds — goes from 10.8 k experts to 46 k (≈7 → 30 GiB) whenever the
demote is on.

**The mechanism, and why it is a bug rather than a tuning miss.**  The exchange headroom is `K = 4 %` of a
table's slots (floor 2, cap slots/8) — ≈19 slots/table at a 500-slot table.  At `auto` the coverage gap is
n_experts − slots = **12 experts per table**.  So `K > gap`: the reserve holds free the very slots the
complement needs, every fill is refused (`pool_free_slot_locked(keep_free = K)`), and each prefill ubatch
re-reads the missing 12 through the **bounce ring** instead of admitting them once.  At `-lme 44` the gap is 32
> K = 19, so the complement admits normally — which is exactly the cell where the demote wins +21 %.  The rule
is structural: **the headroom must never be larger than the coverage gap, and it must never gate the prefill's
fill path.**  `TODO` #281.

**Finding 2 — the ranking is not the field-shape problem.**  `decode` vs `combined` at 97.7 %: prefill 1762.8 vs
1865.3/1883.9 and decode 75.8 vs 77.8/77.9.  Both within the box's spread; A0's 779.1 did not reproduce (A0b,
same config, 1762.8) and is recorded as an outlier.  The r224 ranking default is a wash here; the demote default
is not.

**Finding 3 — 97.7 % coverage costs little against a mirror (+2.2 GiB cheaper); it is the demote that ruins it.**
Mirror 1932.5 / 83.7 vs `auto`+SINK=0 1837 / 76.6: **−5 % prefill, −8 % decode for 2.2 GiB less pinned RAM.**
`auto` is a good enough budget on this box; `auto` + the shipped demote is not.

**Note on the cold/warm page cache (a protocol lesson).**  M1 cold = 1063.6 and M1 warm = 1932.5, same config:
the first 50k pass pays ~870 t/s for faulting the PLE (26.8 GiB, `--lazy-mode auto`) and the mapping into a cold
page cache.  The record's 1941 is the **warm** number, so its 50k rows (and ours) are only comparable warm.  Every
arm after M1 in this pass was warm, and all `auto` arms are therefore comparable with each other.

## 4. The fix (r228, block 12) — `TODO` #281 closed

Three rules, all in `moe-expert-cache.cu` (see `patches/README.md` block 12 and `WORKLOG.md` 261106.019):

1. `K` is capped by the pool's coverage gap (`n_experts - slots`).
2. A **prefill** fill ignores the exchange headroom (`expert_host_src(..., prefill=true)`, `stage_span_fill`);
   a new census line `sink headroom: N prefill fills took the exchange slots` makes it visible.
3. A **near-mirror** pool (coverage gap < 4 % of the set) **disengages** the exchange.

**Measured after the fix (r226 and r227 trees, same box):**

|---|---|---|---|
| field `auto` 97.7 %, first-pass 50k prefill | 867 | **1 907 / 1 938** (two runs, fast drive state) | 1 837 (pre-fix) / 1 938 (post-fix) |
| field `auto`, decode | 76.1 | 75.7 | 75.6-77.7 |
| field census | 49 524 demoted, "neither tier" 46 k | **0 demoted, near-mirror 144, "neither tier" 9 261** | 10 125 |
| `-lme 44` (93.7 %) gen / prompt | 34.2-34.5 / 945-1 028 | **34.5 / 946** | 28.9 / 638 |
| `-lme 36` (76.9 %) gen | 26.0 | **26.0** | 23.8 |
| coherence (prose, pool engaged) | — | `sha=880c932888f8` both arms | same |

So on the field shape the demote is now **the `SINK=0` path to the counter** (it cannot fire above the 96 %
coverage line), and the two cells where it pays are untouched: **+19 % at 94 %** and **+9 % at 77 %**.

**Measurement caveat that cost time twice:** this box's NVMe makes a 0.65 MiB `O_DIRECT` read ~0.65 ms warm and
~10 ms on the first touch after idle, so a first-pass 50k prefill swings **3x** (627 vs 1 938 t/s) with the
drive state, and a per-expert read path (SPAN=0) swings with it.  Always measure the `SINK`/`SPAN` pair
back-to-back and quote the pair, or the census that proves the path.
