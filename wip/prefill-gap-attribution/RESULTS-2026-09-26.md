# RESULTS 2026-09-26 — the prefill gap is GEMM; vllm.cpp is not the path; `cllm` FP8 is

Method: `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace` per-class fold (`tools/fold-kernel-trace.py`), 27B single GPU,
plus a read of `mudler/vllm.cpp` and of `origin/cllm` @ `stew675/llama.cpp`. No delivery code changed.

---

## 1. The attribution (the finding that decides the question)

Across Q8_0 / Q6_K / Q4_K_XL at pp8192, **`mul_mat_q` is 79-81 % of prefill GPU time** and attention
is 3.7-5.5 %. At pp32768 attention rises to 17.1 % but GEMM is still 69.8 %. Full table:
`MEASUREMENTS.md`.

⇒ The single-request prefill gap is a **GEMM-throughput** gap. Any attention-side work (which is what
vllm.cpp actually contributes) is capped at 4-5 % at typical prompt lengths.

## 2. What vllm.cpp contributes, and why it is not viable

* It **builds on gfx1201** (`-DVLLM_CPP_HIP=ON`, `VLLM_CPP_HIP_ARCHITECTURES=gfx1201`, ~11 min).  Its
  own CMake prints `NEVER COMPILED UPSTREAM OF YOU — this skeleton has no build report from any
  machine`, so this is likely the first ROCm build.
* Its useful artifacts are the HIP ports of vLLM/SGLang prefill attention
  (`src/vt/rocm/rocm_paged_attn.hip`: `PagedAttnPrefillFlashTile`, `PagedAttnPrefillWmmaWave`,
  `PagedAttnPrefillSharedK`), plus the vLLM split-KV decode heuristic.
* But: its ROCm backend registers **no FP8 GEMM** (only `kReshapeAndCacheFp8` and
  `rocm_fp8_channel_gemv.hip`), its own gfx1151 survey reads llama.cpp **12.22** > vLLM **11.06** >
  vllm.cpp **5.40** (decode), and its issue #2109 reports its keep-quant GEMM has **no tensor-core arm
  on any RDNA target** and names *llama.cpp's RDNA3 WMMA (`mma.cuh:729`)* as the arm to port.

⇒ vllm.cpp is behind llama.cpp where it matters and cannot address a GEMM-bound gap. **Assessed;
not the path.** Keep the attention ports as a reference only.

## 3. The precision confound (why the ratio is not a pure engine gap)

The 27B FP8 checkpoint is HF safetensors with dynamic-activation e4m3; **llama.cpp has no FP8 type at
all**, so the 75 % figure pits vLLM-FP8 against llama.cpp-int8/Q4_K. See `MEASUREMENTS.md` §3.

## 4. `origin/cllm` — the actual path (prior art already in the house)

A complete FP8 E4M3 port of llama.cpp on RDNA4, 2026-08-06, 22 commits ahead of its merge-base:

* `GGML_TYPE_F8_E4M3` (43), 128-block f32 scale.
* `ggml/src/ggml-cuda/fp8.cu/.cuh`: **aiter-style WMMA fp8 GEMM** (`mul_mat_fp8_wmma`) — CTA 128x64,
  8 warps, `GROUP_M=4` pid swizzle, 16x16x128 tiles, 2 CTAs/CU, register-staged, repacked weights;
  plus `quantize_fp8` activation staging and a `dot4` GEMV for decode.
* `src/llama-safetensors.{cpp,h}` (direct safetensors loading), `--outtype fp8_e4m3` GGUF conversion,
  CPU fp8 get_rows, chunked-GDN rewrite.
* **Measured on Qwen3.5-4B / 1× R9700:** pp512 **7184-7260 t/s vs Q8_0 6177** (+16-17 %), tg64 -1.5 %,
  PPL 6.2250 vs Q8_0 6.2464.  `mul_mat_fp8_wmma` prefill share ~48 %, ~77 TFLOP/s effective.

## 5. AITER — what vLLM actually runs on ROCm, and the remaining gap

`AITER_FINDINGS.md` (on `origin/cllm`) documents that AITER is vLLM's default ROCm kernel backend; on
gfx1201 the CK/ASM paths are CDNA-only and AITER dispatches to **Triton JIT** fp8 blockscale GEMM,
with 30 AMD-tuned configs under `aiter/ops/triton/configs/gemm/gfx1201-GEMM-A8W8_BLOCKSCALE*.json`.

| fp8 GEMM, M=512, 4B shapes | TFLOP/s |
|---|---:|
| AITER (Triton) | 121-137 |
| cllm `mul_mat_fp8_wmma` | 77 (~92-98 hot-loop rig) |
| raw gfx1201 WMMA ceiling | ~370 |

⇒ Reviving `cllm` gives +16 % over Q8_0; lifting AITER's gfx1201 tuning is what could close the rest
(the cllm kernel is at ~21-26 % of raw, AITER at ~33-37 %).

## 6. Recommendation

1. Re-base `origin/cllm` (22 commits / 44 files / +6606 lines over `6ea215d17`; base drift ~7 weeks
   from `84e76d8a2`) and reproduce the 4B +16-17 %.
2. Convert the 27B FP8 safetensors → F8_E4M3 GGUF; measure 27B fp8 prefill vs Q8_0/Q6_K.
3. Port AITER's gfx1201 fp8 GEMM tiles/GROUP_M/config selection into `mul_mat_fp8_wmma`.
4. Archive vllm.cpp as assessed-not-viable; keep `rocm_paged_attn.hip` as the attention reference.
