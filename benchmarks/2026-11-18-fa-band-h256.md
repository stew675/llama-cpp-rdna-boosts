# The 256-dim RDNA4 band: a work gate for the class, and why an adaptive `P` was refuted (r315, block 05, `TODO` #345)

Everything here was measured on the primary box: 3× RX 9070 XT / R9700 (gfx1201), ROCm 10.1.0,
`~/llama-scratch` (one long-lived source path, `build-rocm`), the r314 block-05 chain. `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0`
on every fresh-process comparison (INVARIANTS r44). `nsm` as ggml counts it on this part is **32** (64 CUs), so
the band's default `P` is 32 for the native-quantized K/V types (`nsm`) and 16 for the 2-byte ones (`nsm/2`).

## 1. `K->ne[1]` at the FA op is the PADDED USED LENGTH, not the cache size

This corrects `INVARIANTS.md` r314 item 1 and `GREEDY-PURITY.md` §96 rule 2. A one-shot probe in the band
predicate (27B Q8_0, 3-GPU `-sm tensor`) printing `(head, gqa, Q->ne[1], K->ne[1])` on every change:

| run | what the probe read |
|---|---|
| `-c 8192`, 51-token prompt, `-n 400` | `8192` (graph **reservation** passes: rows 64/1/512) then `256` for every decode |
| `-c 8192`, 5246-token prose prompt, `-n 300` | `768, 1280, …, 3840` during the prompt ubatches, `8192` for the ubatch that crossed `LLAMA_KV_N_PAD_PREFILL_FROM`, then `5376` → `5632` while generating |

`llama_kv_cache::get_n_kv()` returns `min(cells.size(), max(pad, GGML_PAD(used, pad)))` with `pad` = 256 for a
decode/verify batch, so the extent is the padded *used* length and it grows with the context. The `8192` values
are (a) a prompt ubatch under the multi-device prefill pad and (b) the graph-**reservation** context, whose
constructor sets `n_kv = kv->get_size()` — which is where r314's "cache size" reading came from.

Consequences, and they are all in the change:

* The attribute is the right one to price a *work* gate with: it is the number of KV iterations the kernel
  executes (`iter_k = ceil(ne11/nbatch_fa)`).
* A verify batch can straddle a 256-cell padding boundary and take the other kernel family for that one step.
  `test-logits-width-probe` at `P = 9980` (W=1..4 tile, W=5..8 band) **FAILs, maxdiff 0.327**; every arm with a
  constant family passes with maxdiff 0 (see §4). Accepted and recorded — keying on the cache size instead
  would band a shallow context on a large `-c` and reintroduce the losses of §3.
* An adaptive `P` keyed on it is not available (it would move mid-run), which is a second, independent reason
  route (b) below is not implementable as scoped.

## 2. Per op: the band loses at the decode width below ~8192 cells, at EVERY `P`

`test-backend-ops perf -o FLASH_ATTN_EXT`, head 256 / 4 K/V heads, q8_0 and f16 K/V, `us/run`, band ÷ tile
(`GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA=0`). The band column is the best of `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA_SPLIT` = 4/8/16/32/64; the
parenthesised value is the row's optimum.

| GQA | kv | nb | q8_0 | f16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 512 | 1 | **2.64** (P16) | **1.96** (P16) |
| 6 | 1024 | 1 | **2.13** (P32) | **1.59** (P16) |
| 6 | 2048 | 1 | **1.30** (P16) | **1.89** (P16) |
| 6 | 4096 | 1 | **1.26** (P16) | **1.48** (P16) |
| 6 | 8192 | 1 | 0.96 (P16) | **1.09** (P16) |
| 6 | 32768 | 1 | 0.66 (P16) | **1.10** (P8) |
| 6 | 512 | 8 | **1.14** (P16) | **1.24** (P8) |
| 6 | 1024 | 8 | 0.76 (P16) | 0.71 (P8) |
| 6 | 2048 | 8 | 0.39 (P16) | 0.47 (P8) |
| 6 | 4096 | 8 | 0.31 (P16) | 0.40 (P8) |
| 6 | 8192 | 8 | 0.25 (P16) | 0.33 (P8) |
| 6 | 32768 | 8 | 0.18 (P8) | 0.25 (P8) |

Bold = the band is slower. The other ratios at their default `P` (q8_0): GQA 8 **2.46** at 1024/nb1, 1.56 at
8192/nb1, 0.45 at 8192/nb8; GQA 12 **2.54** at 1024/nb1, **1.65** at 4096, 1.12 at 16384, 0.91 at 32768, 0.34
at 8192/nb8.

So the 256-dim band is a **verify-width** kernel: it wins from 1024-2048 cells at `nb` 4..8 and it loses at the
decode width until ~8192 cells, non-monotonically and never (for f16) beyond it.

  `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA_H256=1` (the band itself, which is now the band's own oracle: under `auto` every
  short-cache case takes the tile kernel), `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA=0` (tile) and `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA=2`.
* `test-logits-width-probe` on the 27B (P = prefix length; `cells = PAD(P+W, 256)`, so the gate's own band
  selection starts at P ≈ 9928 for GQA 6):

|---|---|---|
| `auto` (tile: 3840/4352 cells), q8_0 / f16 / bf16 | 3584, 4096 | PASS, worst maxdiff 0 |

## 5. Route (b), adaptive `P`, refuted — and a separate tuning finding

`TODO` #345 route (b) was `P = min(nsm, ceil(cells / nbatch_fa))`. Both halves of its premise are wrong:

* **At depth there are no idle blocks to remove.** 32768 cells / `nbatch_fa` 64 = 512 KV iterations against
  `P` = 32 = `nsm`: every block owns 16 of them, so the rule returns the current value.
* **At a short cache the idle blocks cost nothing.** A block that owns no iteration writes the neutral partial
  (`max -FLT_MAX/2`, rowsum 0) and exits; halving `P` instead serialises the KV iterations with no more
  parallelism to hide them. Measured at 512 cells (GQA 6, nb1): `P` 8 = 52.6 µs, `P` 16 = 31.1, `P` 32 = 39.2,
  `P` 64 = 56.6, tile 15.8 — the adaptive rule picks 8, the worst of the four.
* `P` cannot be keyed on the cache size either (§1: there is no such attribute at the op).

**Separate finding, not shipped here:** `P` = 16 is the optimum in 30 of the 36 measured rows, and the
quantized default `P = nsm` (32) is 10-20 % off it at most sizes (512 `P16` 42.8 vs `P32` 63.3; 8192 59.7 vs
68.8; 32768 146.8 vs 158.3), with one anomalous row (1024 nb1 q8_0: 53.3 vs 42.7). A default-`P` change moves
the numerics of *every* banded model (both head classes), so it needs its own item and its own gate ladder; it
is recorded in `TODO.md` rather than folded into this change.

## 6. Not validated

gfx1100 / gfx1151 (`fingon` / `halo`) and Windows were not exercised for this arm; the change is a selection
rule on already-validated kernels and its oracle is the same suite in both arms. A **non-SWA** 256-dim model
whose whole run sits below the gate was not available — the class's members on this box (27B, 35B-A3B,
Flash-Next) are all weight-bound at that depth, which is what the gate is for.
