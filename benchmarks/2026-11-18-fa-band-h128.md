# The RDNA4 decode/verify band for 128-dim heads with an odd GQA ratio (r314, block 05, contributor PR #329)

Everything here was measured on the primary box: 3× RX 9070 XT / R9700 (gfx1201), ROCm 10.1.0,
`~/llama-scratch` (one long-lived source path, `build-rocm`), kernel `rcut314`/`b6ff0a958` (the r314 block
05 amendment).  `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0` on every arm (INVARIANTS r44).  The submission's own numbers are
from the contributor's single R9700 on ROCm 10.0.0; its claims reproduce here to within the arm-to-arm
spread quoted below.

## 1. Per-op: band / tile-kernel time, the decode width, and the crossover

`test-backend-ops perf -o FLASH_ATTN_EXT`, band on (`GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA_H128=1`) against the tile kernel
(`=0`), q8_0 K/V, 8 K/V heads, `us/run`.  `< 1` is a band win; **bold** rows are where the unconditional
submission was SLOWER than the tile kernel it replaces.

| ratio | 512 | 1024 | 2048 | 4096 | 8192 | 16384 | 32768 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5  | **1.67** | **1.43** | **1.26** | 0.94 | 0.65 | 0.51 | 0.48 |
| 7  | **1.44** | **1.20** | **1.05** | 0.72 | 0.48 | 0.37 | 0.35 |
| 10 | **2.33** | **1.79** | **1.70** | **1.24** | 0.96 | 0.81 | 0.77 |
| 12 | **2.47** | **2.08** | **1.83** | **1.45** | **1.14** | 0.95 | 0.91 |

Verify widths tilt the other way (ratio 10, `nb` 4 / 8): 0.81 / 0.65 at 1024, 0.61 / 0.48 at 2048, 0.41 / 0.34
at 4096.  So the rule has to be a *work* test, not a cell count: a flat 8192 would enable ratio 12 at 8192
(a 1.14 loss) and deny ratio 7 at 4096 (a 0.72 win).

**The gate.**  `K->ne[1] * (ceil(ratio/ncols2_tile) - ceil(ratio/8)) >= GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_H128_MIN_WORK`
(default 20000), where `ncols2_tile` is the largest power of two dividing the ratio capped at 8 (what the tile
dispatcher picks).  That reads 16384 / 24576 / 12288 / 4096 at 4096 cells for ratios 5 / 7 / 10 / 12 and
32768 / 49152 / 24576 / 8192 at 8192 -- i.e. it lands on the measured crossover in every row, and the decision
boundary measured by timing is band-from **8192 (r5), 4096 (r7), 8192 (r10), 32768 (r12)**.

`K->ne[1]` is the layer's KV **cache size**, not the used length: a one-shot print in the predicate read
`kv=8192` for the global layers at `-c 8192` and `kv=16384` at `-c 16384` for the *same* 51-token prompt, and
`kv=1536` (= `GGML_PAD(min(size_base, n_swa + n_ubatch), 256)`, `n_swa` 1024 + `-ub` 512) for the windowed
layers of a ricursive model.  See `INVARIANTS.md` r314 item 1 for why a used-length test cannot be used.

GQA 8 (128-dim, the class the change must NOT touch -- same kernel instances, +37 VGPRs of band code): every
op case within 0.998-1.002 of the pre-change build.

## 2. End to end: the one 128-dim odd-ratio model on this box

`/llm/models/Ripple-65B/Q5_K_M/Ripple-65B-Preview-AD-Q5_K_M.gguf` -- ricursive 65B, 80 Q / 8 KV = **GQA 10**,
head 128, iSWA with **64 of 80 layers windowed at 1024** cells.  `llama-bench -p 0 -n 64 -r 3`, 3 GPUs
`-sm tensor`, f16 K/V, `-fa 1`, interleaved `A B A B`-style pairs (two runs per arm), tg64 t/s:

| depth | auto (gated) | tile (`H128=0`) | unconditional (`H128=1`) | auto vs tile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0     | 28.38 / 28.31 | 28.32 | 26.54 / 26.78 | **+0.1 %** |
| 4096  | 27.41 / 27.39 | 27.39 | 26.01 / 25.97 | **+0.1 %** |
| 16384 | 26.66 / 26.68 | 26.91 | 25.57 / 25.58 | **-0.9 %** |
| 32768 | 26.16 / 26.16 | 26.30 | 25.14 / 25.12 | **-0.5 %** |

The unconditional arm is the submission's default: **-5.8 / -5.1 / -4.9 / -4.5 %** at every depth, because 64
of 80 layers read a 1536-cell cache no matter how deep the context is.  With the work gate those layers stay
on the tile kernel (0 % at d0/d4K, where the *global* layers are also below the crossover at ratio 10) and the
residual -0.5..-0.9 % at d16K/d32K is the 16 global layers: at ratio 10 the band only folds 5 passes into 2
(op 0.77 at 32768), and a 44.6 GiB dense model at 28 t/s (1.24 TB/s of weight traffic per token) has no spare
GPU time for a 23 % win on one op.  `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA_H128=0` is the documented answer for that model.

Text: `-c 2048` (the gate declines every layer -- 1536 cells x 3 < 20000) reproduces r313 byte for byte,
`146 chars sha=c1e00039b55c` for 128 greedy tokens; at `-c 8192` the 16 global layers do take the band, which
is the same numeric arm as any other band class (coincidentally the same 128 tokens here).

## 3. Correctness and numerics

* `test-backend-ops -o FLASH_ATTN_EXT`, `auto` **7381/7381**, `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA_H128=1` **7381/7381**,
  `=0` and `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA=2` **7333/7333** (the same case list before the last 48 band cases were
  added).  The list carries 384 new 128-dim cases: ratios 5/6/7/12 x 8 and 4 K/V heads x widths 1..16, q8_0
  and f16 at kv 512/1024/4096 (a 256-cell masked group at 4096, i.e. the packed hole), plus q8_0/f16/**bf16**
  at kv 32768 -- past the gate for every ratio, and the band's first bf16 oracle at this head size.
* `test-logits-width-probe` on ricursive 65B, `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA_H128=1`, P=4096: **width purity PASS,
  worst maxdiff 0** with `KV=f16`, `bf16` and `q8_0`.  The mixed configuration the gate creates (P=8192,
  windowed layers on tile and global layers on band): **PASS**, maxdiff 0.
* KLD, `gate-prefill-logits.sh --family-switch --ub 4 --chunks 8`, 3 GPUs `-sm tensor`, band (`=1`) against
  tile (`=0`): **mean 0.001013 / same-top-p 99.412 %** against a same-kernel `-ub 256` control of
  **0.002222 / 98.676 %**.  Both inside the absolute limits, and the band's own divergence is smaller than a
  plain ubatch change -- the submission's KLD claim holds.
* Coherence (3-GPU `-sm tensor`, `-n 96`, `--seed 42 --temp 0`): 4B `8d89080db673`, 27B `da2e2d192e21`,
  35B-A3B `ae39ec41bc1e` -- all byte-identical to r313.
* MTP four axes (`draft-mtp n3`, `-n 512`, dense 27B Q8_0, reasoning off except R): acceptance **identical to
  r313** on every axis -- K 321/564 (0.56915), R 349/485 (0.71959), C 370/422 (0.87678), P 352/474 (0.74262).
* GPU kernel journal and every run log clean (`scripts/check-gpu-faults.sh`).

## 4. Not validated

A **non-SWA** 128-dim odd-ratio model at a real context (the submission's Nanbeige4.3-33B / Qwen2.5 case):
there is no such file on this box, so its +16 % at 16K / +36 % at 32K is quoted from the contributor and
corroborated only at the op level (0.48-0.65 for ratio 5/7 from 8192 cells).  gfx1100 / gfx1151 and Windows
are untested for this arm; so is `--swa-full` (which raises a windowed layer's cache to the full context, so
the gate would then enable the band on layers that read mostly-masked rows).

## 5. The 256-dim arm, measured for the scoping of `TODO` #345 (NOT changed by r314)

The r314 gate is deliberately 128-dim only.  The 256-dim arm has the same per-layer cost profile and is
unconditional (default-on since r6), so it was measured to scope the follow-up rather than to change it.

**Per op** (q8_0, decode width, band / tile via `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA=0`): GQA 6 **3.94** at 512 cells,
**2.05** at 1024, **1.70** at 2048, **1.51** at 4096, then ~parity at depth (**1.01** at 16384, **1.00** at
65536, **1.00** at 131072, f16); GQA 8 2.94 / 2.18 / 1.94 / 1.97 / 1.02 / 1.00.  Verify widths win early
(GQA 6 at 1024 cells: 1.26 at `nb` 4, 0.92 at `nb` 8; at 4096: 0.53 / 0.44).  So unlike the 128-dim class the
256-dim band is only *clearly* better at the verify widths and at shallow depth it is 1.5-3.9x worse.

**End to end**, Qwen3.8-27B Q8_0 (head 256, GQA 6), 3 GPUs `-sm tensor`, `ROCBLAS_USE_HIPBLASLT=0`, tg64,
`llama-bench -r 3` -- band default against `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WMMA=0`:

| depth | band | tile | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 44.04 ± 1.03 | 46.18 ± 0.39 | **-4.6 %** |
| 4096 | 44.97 ± 0.58 | 45.69 ± 0.59 | **-1.6 %** |
| 8192 | 44.49 ± 0.62 | 45.17 ± 0.61 | **-1.5 %** |
| 16384 | 43.59 ± 0.56 | 44.31 ± 0.56 | **-1.6 %** |
| 32768 | 41.93 ± 0.52 | 42.64 ± 0.51 | **-1.7 %** |

and with a drafter (`draft-mtp n3`, 5246-token prose prompt, `-c 8192`, `-n 512`): band **107.4 t/s** against
tile **112.7 t/s**, i.e. **-4.7 %**.  A 27.3 B Q8_0 model decoding at ~44 t/s is reading ~1.2 TB/s of weights
per token -- at the box's DRAM roof -- so a *faster attention op* buys nothing and the band's per-layer launch
cost is paid in full.  (The same effect is why the 128-dim arm on ricursive 65B still reads -0.5..-0.9 % at
depth after its gate.)

**What this means for #345**: the 256-dim question is not "gate it on cache size" -- the op is at parity at
depth and the end-to-end is negative wherever the model is weight-bound, so a cache-size gate would fix only
the shallow cases and leave the deep ones at -1.6 %.  The candidate routes are (a) the same work gate (cheap,
fixes 512-4096, leaves depth alone), (b) an adaptive `P = min(nsm, ceil(cells/nbatch_fa))`, which keeps the
fold and stops the idle blocks -- legal because `P` may depend on the run-constant cache size even though it
may not depend on the used length -- and (c) leave the default and document, since every published 256-dim
band win was measured on arms where attention *is* the bottleneck (deep single-GPU prefill/verify), not on a
weight-bound 3-GPU decode.  Each route moves kernel selection for every GQA 5..12 256-dim model, so each needs
the full band ladder (width purity at P 3584/4096, the four MTP axes, `gate-prefill-logits`, `-sm tensor` and
`-sm layer` arms, and the `GGML_HIP_FA_BAND_WIDE` interaction at `K->ne[1] >= 4096`).
